## Borderlands SHiFT Codes v1.8.0

![Borderlands SHiFT Codes Banner](/images/BorderlandsSHiFTCodes/Banner.png)

I'm excited to announce the release of **Borderlands SHiFT Codes v1.8.0**. This release improves startup stability and sync reliability when loading newly added codes.

### About the Application

Borderlands SHiFT Codes is an unofficial fan project designed to provide easy access to SHiFT codes for the Borderlands and Wonderlands gaming universe. This Android application serves as a convenient way to view and manage codes that can be redeemed on the official Gearbox SHiFT website or directly in-game.

### What's New in v1.8.0

Fixed intermittent startup issue where newly added codes could briefly appear twice. Corrected the issue by making sure codes are unique during remote/local reconciliation and by adding sync serialization to reduce race conditions during startup/background refresh overlap.
