## Borderlands SHiFT Codes v1.7.0

![Borderlands SHiFT Codes Banner](/images/BorderlandsSHiFTCodes/Banner.png)

I'm excited to announce the release of **Borderlands SHiFT Codes v1.7.0**.  This release fixes a small issue with some SHiFT codes being truncated on the compact view.  Corrected this by removing the status dot on the compact view.  The status dot was redundant, as the compact cards already had a colored bar on the left to indicate status.

### About the Application

Borderlands SHiFT Codes is an unofficial fan project designed to provide easy access to SHiFT codes for the Borderlands and Wonderlands gaming universe. This Android application serves as a convenient way to view and manage codes that can be redeemed on the official Gearbox SHiFT website or directly in-game.

### What's New in v1.7.0

Removed the redundant status dot in compact view to prevent SHiFT code text from being truncated.
